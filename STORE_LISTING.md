# Google Play store listing and declarations (draft)

## Listing
**App name (max 30):** LPMS – Literacy Programme
**Short description (max 80):** Run literacy sessions, attendance and progress for READ schools, teachers and parents.

**Full description:**
LPMS is the companion app for the Literacy Programme Management System used with READ Educational Trust.

- Teachers: see your learners, schedule sessions, mark attendance, record reading progress and message parents.
- Coordinators: manage sessions and schools, enrol learners, link parents, log school visits and export funder reports.
- Admins: approve new accounts, manage roles and review the activity log.
- Parents: follow your child's attendance and reading level, and message the teacher.
- Works with patchy signal: changes made offline are saved on the phone and uploaded later.
- Children's records are visible only to authorised staff, the learner's teacher and linked parents.

Sign-up for teachers and parents is approved by READ staff.

## Data safety answers
- Data collected: Name, email address, user IDs, other in-app content (attendance, reading progress, messages).
- Purpose: app functionality and account management. Not used for advertising or analytics. Not shared with third parties (Firebase acts as processor).
- Encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: Yes (contact the programme admin / support email).
- Data about children: held for programme administration only; made visible to staff, the school's teacher and linked parents.

## Declarations
- App type: App. Free. Ads: No.
- Target audience: 18+ (staff, teachers, parents). Not designed for children.
- Content rating: no violence, no user-generated public content (private messages only). Expect an "Everyone" rating.
- App access: restricted; provide demo login (e.g. teacher@school.org and its password).
- Privacy policy URL: required, host a page covering the data above (a GitHub Pages page is fine).
